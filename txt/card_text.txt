Original cards of physical game (spanish):

/-------
Cartas:
/-------
#ERROR*:
	Si te pasaste este turno, avanza 6 casillas
Trucar juego:
	Aumenta en 2 el máximo de tu tirada este turno (el premio sigue siendo 10 o más)
Apuesta:
	Si jugaste esta carta antes de empezar tu tirada, obtienes el doble de premios
Trueque:
	Dale una carta a otro jugador, el deberá darte 2 cartas (puede ser la que le diste)
¡Combo!:
	Avanza 3 casillas por cada otra carta que hayas jugado este turno
Perdición:
	Si un jugador tiene exactamente 7 en su tirada, acaba su turno
Terremoto:
	Todos los demás jugadores tiran un dado y retroceden la cantidad que obtuvieron
Videncia
	Todos los jugadores te muestran sus cartas en secreto
Secretaria de Broadway:
	Si jugaste otras 3 cartas este turno, roba 2 cartas
Embargo:
	Un jugador sin cartas retrocede 10 casillas
Az en la manga:
	Si esta es la ultima carta en tu mano, avanza 7 casillas
Albedrío:
	Antes de conseguir un premio, elige entre las primeras 3 opciones
¡Feliz cumpleaños!:
	Si tienes otras 6 cartas obtiene un premio
Escaleras:
	Todos los jugadores en casilleros pares avanzan 8 casillas
Serpientes:
	Todos los jugadores en casilleros impares retroceden 7 casillas
Robo al casino:
	Todos los jugadores obtienen un premio
Agotado:
	Nadie puede obtener premios hasta tu próximo turno
Pase libre:
	Avanza 4 casillas
Enchufe:
	Añade 2 a tu tirada
Tragaperras:
	Tira el dado, avanza esa cantidad de casillas
Tarjeta Amarilla:
	Si jugaste otra tarjeta amarilla este turno, un jugador retrocede 29 casillas
Mala leche:
	Arrastra a un jugador a tu posición, despues retrocede 6 casillas
Seda el paso:
	Ningún jugador puede jugar cartas hasta tu proximo turno
Vacaciones:
	Ningún jugador puede robar cartas hasta tu proximo turno
Rebobinar:
	Reduce en 1 el resultado de tu tirada, incluse si te pasaste (pudiendo anular el pasarse)
Muro de hielo portatil:
	Hasta tu próximo turno, cualquier jugador que fuera a alcanzarte se quedea una casilla atras de ti
Envidia:
	Todos los jugadores con mas cartas que tu antes de jugar esta, descartan 2 cartas
Confiscar:
	Anula el efecto de otra carta
Cuidador de cementerio:
	Toma la carta de encima de la pila de descartes
Limite de velocidad:
	Hasta tu proximo turno los jugadores no pueden avanzar mas de 5 casillas
Reciclaje:
	Roba una carta
Sacrifico a C'thulhu:
	Descarta otras 2 cartas para obtener un premio

/-------
Premios:
/-------
Pase VIP:
	Mueve a un jugador a la casilla 57
Auto 0km:
	Avanze 13 casillas
La caja de Mi-Go:
	Teletransportate a la posición de otro jugador, o roba 2 cartas
Avaricia:
	Los demás jugadores restroceden 5 casillas por cada carta en su mano
Combinado Inestable:
	Juega inmediatamente las primeras 4 cartas del mazo en ese orden
Colisionador Temporal:
	Juega otro turno
Tres deseos:
	Roba 3 cartas
Bancarrota:
	Los demás jugadores descartan 3 cartas
Guantes de ladron:
	Roba dos cartas de otro jugador (tu las eliges sin verlas)
Tarjeta Roja:
	Un jugador retrocede 29 casillas
Festin:
	Los demás jugadores roban 2 cartas, tu robas 5 cartas

English translation:

/-------
Cards:
/-------
#ERROR*:
    If you failed a spin this turn, move forward 6 spaces
Cheat game:
	Increase your maximum spin by 2 this turn (the prize is still 10 or more)
Bet:
	If you played this card before starting your spin, you get double the prizes on settle
Trade:
	Give a card to another player; they must give you 2 cards (it can be the one you gave them)
Combo!:
	Move forward 3 cells for every other card you've played this turn
Doom:
	If another player rolls exactly 7, their turn ends
Earthquake:
	All other players roll a die and move back the number they rolled
Clairvoyance:
	All players secretly show you their hands
Broadway Secretary:
	If you played 3 other cards this turn, draw 2 cards
Expropriation:
	A player with no cards moves back 10 spaces
Ace up your sleeve:
	If this is the last card in your hand, move forward 7 spaces
Free will:
	Before claiming a prize, choose from the first 3 options
Happy Birthday!:
	If you have 6 other cards, win a prize
Snakes:
	All players on odd numbered cells move back 7 cells
Ladders:
	All players on even numbered spaces move forward 8 cells
Casino heist:
	All players win a prize
Sold Out:
	No one can win prizes until your next turn
Free pass:
	Move forward 4 cells
Plug:
	Add 2 to your spin
Slot machine:
	Roll the dice; move forward that many spaces
Yellow card:
	If you played another yellow card this turn, one player moves back 29 cells
Bad blood [Bad milk]:
	Drag a player to your position, then move back 6 cells
Yield sign:
	No player may play cards until your next turn
Vacation:
	No player may draw cards until your next turn
Rewind:
	Reduce the result of your spin by 1, even if you spinned over (which may cancel out spinning over)
Portable Ice Wall:
	Until your next turn, any player who would catch up to yuo remains one space behind you
Envy:
	All players with more cards than you before playing this card must discard 2 cards
Confiscate:
	Cancels the effect of another card
Graveyard Keeper:
	Take the top card from the discar pile
Speed Limit:
	Until your next turn, players cannot move more that 5 spaces at a time
Recycle:
    Draw a card
Sacrifice to C'thulhu:
    Discard 2 other cards to receive a prize


/-------
Prizes:
/-------
VIP pass:
	Move a player to cell 57
0 km car:
	move forward 13 cells
Mi-Go's box:
	Teleport to another player's position or draw 2 cards
Greed:
	The other players move back 5 cells for each card in their hand
Unstable concoction:
	Immediately play the top 4 cards of the deck in that order
Temporal Collider:
	Play another turn
Three Wishes:
	Draw 3 cards
Bankruptcy:
	The other players discard 3 cards
Thief's gloves:
	Steal 2 cards from another player (you choose them without looking)
Red card:
	A player moves back 29 cells
Feast:
	The other players draw 2 cards; you draw 5 cards.

/-------	
History
/-------

This file seems good enough to state some sort of "version history"
From what I remember, "Envy" was the 30th card. then I had made 10 prizes and 30 cards. I wanted to have 32 cards becouse 2^5
I noticed there where to many disadvantages to having a lot of cards (I was thinking mainly of "Greed" and "Bankruptcy").
So "Happy birthday" went from 7 other cards to 6 and I added "Expropiation", to also punish having no cards.
Then for the 32nd card I thought of cards playing more cards and made "Casino heist", which also made me think of "Unstable concoction".
With 32 cards and 11 prizes now I made the physical game and tested it for the first time.
We played with a lot of players (like 9) and it went pretty bad. But at a later date I tried with only 3 and it worked ok enough.
